🌿 The Science Inside

Key Ingredients

Every Dermec product is crafted with carefully selected, science-backed ingredients.

🧈
Shea Butter

Rich in fatty acids and vitamins, Shea Butter deeply nourishes and protects delicate skin — a gold-standard moisturiser used in our Kidz Bar.

⚗️
Syndet Base

Synthetic detergent base with pH 5.5 — mirrors baby's natural skin pH for the gentlest possible cleanse, unlike traditional alkaline soaps.

🌿
Natural Plant Oils

Cold-pressed plant oils in our massage formulas strengthen skin, support healthy development, and provide a sensory bonding experience.

💧
Moisturising Complex

A blend of humectants and emollients that lock in moisture for 24 hours — keeping skin soft, supple, and hydrated through the day.

🔬
Mild Surfactants

Specially selected surfactants that cleanse without stripping — tested dermatologically to be safe even on the most sensitive skin types.

🛡️
Zero Harsh Chemicals

No parabens, no phthalates, no mineral oils, no artificial dyes — just pure, safe formulations you can trust for your family.
